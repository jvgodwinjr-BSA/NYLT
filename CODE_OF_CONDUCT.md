# Code of conduct

This project supports a Scouting course. The Scout Oath and Law are the standard here, same as at camp.

## What we expect

- **Be trustworthy with information.** Personal information about youth and staff does not belong in this repository, in issues, or in screenshots. See [docs/YOUTH-PROTECTION.md](docs/YOUTH-PROTECTION.md).
- **Be friendly and courteous.** Most people here are volunteers, and some are youth. Review the code, not the person. Assume the other person is doing their best with the time they have.
- **Be helpful.** If you spot something wrong, say what you would do instead. "This is broken" is less useful than one sentence about what you expected.
- **Be kind about mistakes.** Someone will eventually commit something they should not have. The right response is to help fix it quickly, not to make them feel foolish.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments. Personal or political attacks. Publishing anyone's private information. Sexualized language or imagery. Any conduct that would be out of place in front of a Scout — because it may well be in front of one.

## Youth participation

Youth staff are welcome to contribute. Adults interacting with youth through this project follow the same Youth Protection expectations as anywhere else in Scouting: keep communication in channels that others can see, include another adult, and route anything that belongs in a private conversation through the Course Director rather than a direct message.

## If something goes wrong

Raise it with the repository owner or the NYLT Course Director through normal council channels. Anything involving a youth's safety or personal information goes to the Course Director the same day, and follows council and BSA reporting requirements — which take precedence over anything written here.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block accounts, when this standard is not met.
